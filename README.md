## **About the Game**
Rock Bottom isn’t just a platformer—it’s a symbolic reinvention of something ordinary. We took the simplest element—a rock—and gave it life, turning a mundane action like rolling and jumping into a metaphor for growth, struggle, and recovery.

You control a small stone that fell into a deep pit. To escape, you’ll roll, jump, and fight your way upward through stages that reflect the emotional phases of recovery:

⚫ Rock Bottom 
🔴 Anger 
🟢 Confusion 
🔵 Sadness 
🟡 Acceptance
